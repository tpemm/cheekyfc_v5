# Team playstyle foundation

The Playstyle tab consumes transparent team attack and defense profiles derived from observed match rows. Supported future rates include goals, xG/xGA, shots/SOT, key passes, fantasy points, and Ghost allowed. Metrics absent from actual sources are omitted. There is no composite Playstyle, Attack, or Defense score.

Sofascore formations and match statistics are unavailable in soccerdata 1.9.1, and WhoScored lacks a proven cache. Formation history therefore remains unbuilt rather than inferred from player eligibility.
# Sprint 9.2 supplemental boundary

Formation and event-derived inputs are proven for one historical WhoScored
match. They remain POC-only; no single-match formation share or team-style
classification is presented as season truth.
