# Tactical role taxonomy

Fantrax eligibility and football role are separate dimensions. Raw WhoScored
roles are always preserved. The conservative initial vocabulary is:

`GK; LB, LCB, CB, RCB, RB; LWB, RWB; DM, LDM, RDM; LCM, CM, RCM; LM, RM;
CAM, LAM, RAM; LW, RW; SS, CF, ST`.

The real sample maps exact codes `DR→RB`, `DC→CB`, `DL→LB`, `DMC→DM`,
`AMC→CAM`, `AML→LW`, `AMR→RW`, and `FW→ST`. `Sub` is not a tactical role.
Unknown labels remain unresolved rather than being guessed.

Across eight matches `DMR` and `DML` occur in back-three structures and map
conservatively to RWB and LWB. Every non-Sub raw code in the sample maps.
