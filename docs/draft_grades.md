# 2026/27 Draft Grades

Completed-draft grading is generated by `scripts/build_draft_grades.py` from the registered completed draft and an immutable draft-day rankings snapshot. Grades measure decisions using information available at draft time; they are not in-season hindsight evaluations.

## Inputs, roster, and snapshot

The authoritative record is `data/imports/draft/draft_results_2627.csv`: 180 picks, 12 managers, 15 rounds, and 15 selections per manager. Validation rejects errors rather than repairing them. The first build freezes `draft_rankings_2627.csv` at `data/snapshots/draft_2627/draft_rankings_draft_day_2627.csv` with adjacent UTC/SHA-256 metadata; later builds reuse it. Routine refreshes do not target this path.

The configured league has 16 roster slots, but the completed draft had 15 selections. Grading uses the actual 11 active plus four bench selections; the 16th configured slot is IR-only. Maximum-cardinality assignment uses Fantrax multi-position eligibility without double-counting players.

## Matching and unresolved review

Priority is exact normalized name plus club, unique exact name, approved draft alias, then initial/surname constrained by club and position. Ambiguous identities remain unresolved and block grading. The match report retains stable IDs, method, confidence, and conflicting evidence.

## Formulas

Pick Grade is separate from unchanged Draft Score: Draft Quality 30%, Market Value 25%, Model Value 15%, Tier Value 10%, Roster Fit 10%, Minutes Security 5%, and Data Confidence 5%. Missing inputs are removed and weights renormalized; missing ADP is never 999 in grading.

Letters: A+ 97, A 93, A- 90, B+ 87, B 83, B- 80, C+ 77, C 73, C- 70, D+ 67, D 63, D- 60, F below 60.

Round weights: rounds 1–3 1.50; 4–6 1.25; 7–10 1.00; 11–13 0.80; 14–15 0.65.

Manager Grade: Player Quality 20%, Value 20%, Projection 15%, Floor 10%, Attacking Upside 10%, Minutes Security 10%, Roster Construction 10%, and Club/Fixture Context 5%. Missing metrics are renormalized. Labels, identities, and awards are deterministic.

## Calibration audit

`raw_analytical_score` preserves the reconciled weighted result. It combines absolute frozen-model aggregates with a league-percentile Projection category, so its league mean is not intended to be a school-grade midpoint. `league_relative_score` is used only for presentation and letters:

`league_relative_score = clip(80 + 10 × population_z_score(raw_analytical_score), 55, 98)`

Exact ties remain ties; zero-variance leagues receive 80; raw ranking and values remain unchanged. Standard z-score was selected after comparison with median/MAD robust z-score and percentile-band mapping. Standard and robust results were nearly identical for this league, while percentile mapping imposed spacing unrelated to actual score separation.

Each category also retains its raw score and adds league rank, percentile, standard-z calibrated category score, and letter. The calibration audit CSV records min, max, mean, median, standard deviation, reconciliation contribution, and all three alternative calibrations. Roster Construction currently measures legal maximum-cardinality completion; all 12 completed legal assignments scored 100, making it valid but non-discriminating for this draft. It is documented rather than silently replaced with a new formula.

Canonical/model outputs live under `data/models/draft_2627`, validation and matching under `data/quality/draft_results_2627`, and shareable outputs under `reports`.

## Sprint 6.1 team grading

Manager grading now uses seven fantasy-facing categories. Pick Grades and their formula remain unchanged.

- Projected Production (20%): league-relative composite of total, average, best-legal-XI, and bench projected points.
- Historical Production (15%): minute-weighted fantasy points per 90.
- Floor (15%): minute-weighted ghost points per 90, average player ghost percentile, and Minutes Confidence.
- Playing Time Security (15%): historical Start %, historical Minutes %, projected Minutes %, Minutes Confidence, starter-outlook mix, rotation, and unknown-player counts.
- Draft Value (15%): pick versus ADP, pick versus Draft HQ Rank, steals, reaches, and average value gained. Missing ADP remains missing.
- Attacking Upside (10%): minute-weighted xGI/90, average xGI percentile, and attacking percentile.
- Roster Construction (10%): projected best legal XI, bench projection, positional balance, multi-position flexibility, starter quality, weakest starter, first bench, and goalkeeper timing.

Every component is converted to a within-league percentile in the correct direction before category aggregation. The legal starting XI is a maximum-projection assignment across the configured G/D/M/F and flex requirements; one player can fill only one slot. Four unselected players form the separately graded bench. Position-group reports cover goalkeeper, defense, midfield, and forward metrics and grades.

Draft HQ presents fantasy-question methodology, headline statistics, category report cards, the projected XI, bench depth, position groups, a league radar profile, and Projection/Floor, Value/ADP, and Upside/Minutes scatterplots.

## Simple league share page

The first completed-draft view is a plain-language League Draft Rankings page. It shows rank, grade, league-relative score, projected starting-XI points, average player projection, average numeric ADP, last-season fantasy and ghost points per 90, historical Start %, projected Minutes %, Main Strength, and Main Concern. Raw analytical scores and category mechanics remain in detailed views.

- Projected Starting XI Points is the sum of Fantrax projections for the optimized legal XI.
- Average Player Projection is the mean projection across all 15 drafted players.
- Average ADP excludes missing ADP; it never substitutes 999.
- Fantasy Points / 90 and Ghost Points / 90 are minute-weighted roster rates: summed points × 90 divided by summed positive historical minutes.
- Historical Start % is appearance-opportunity weighted: total historical starts divided by total available player-weeks for rows with a positive denominator.
- Projected Minutes % is the unweighted roster mean of the authoritative 2026/27 projected-minutes share.
- Main Strength is the manager's best league rank across eight named headline statistics; Main Concern is the corresponding weakest rank. Ties resolve in the documented stable statistic order, producing deterministic plain-English labels.

Detailed category grades, position reports, raw scores, and methodology remain in League Rankings and Manager Report Cards. Standalone `draft_grades_2627_share.csv` and responsive `draft_grades_2627_share.html` reports mirror the landing page.

## League draft poster

`scripts/generate_draft_poster.py` creates a deterministic 2400 × 3200 Pillow poster at `reports/draft_grades_2627_poster.png` plus a PDF export. The layout is three columns by four rows, ordered by calibrated league rank. Draft Grades previews the PNG and exposes PNG/PDF downloads from registered manager and pick datasets; the view does not read raw source files.

Every card uses the same six radar axes: Projected XI, Draft Value, minute-weighted Fantasy/90, minute-weighted Ghost/90, minute-weighted xGI/90, and Starting XI Minutes. Starting XI Minutes is the mean authoritative `projected_minutes_share` across the 11 optimized legal starters. Bench players are excluded, missing values are omitted rather than zero-filled, and the presentation dataset retains coverage plus Locked Starter, Likely Starter, and Rotation/Unknown counts. The source field is already a 0–100 percentage and is not rescaled. Coverage below eight starters is flagged internally.

Each raw statistic is retained and printed beneath the radar. Radar values are within-league average-rank percentiles from 0–100, with higher always better and exact ties sharing ranks/percentiles. Missing ADP remains missing and is excluded from team Draft Value.

Starting position groups reuse the maximum-projection legal XI. Flex starters retain their optimized-XI canonical assignment. Goalkeeper is combined with defense, every group contains unique assigned players, and the remaining four players form Bench.

- Defense + Goalkeeper: projection 45%, Fantasy/90 20%, Ghost/90 15%, projected minutes 15%, confidence 5%.
- Midfield and Forwards: projection 40%, Fantasy/90 20%, Ghost/90 10%, xGI/90 15%, projected minutes 10%, confidence 5%.
- Bench: projection 55%, projected minutes 20%, Fantasy/90 15%, multi-position flexibility 10%.
- Starting XI: projected XI points 45%, Fantasy/90 15%, Ghost/90 15%, xGI/90 10%, Starting XI Minutes 10%, confidence 5%.

Each component is converted to a within-position league percentile before weighting. Bars show the resulting league percentile, exact rank, and label: ranks 1–2 Elite, 3–4 Strong, 5–8 League Average, 9–10 Below Average, and 11–12 Weak.

Identity tags are deterministic and capped at four. They use transparent rank triggers: rank-one projection/value, top-three floor/upside/minutes, top-two position units, and bottom-three minutes or bench. Mutually exclusive Minutes Secure/Rotation Risk and Strong Bench/Thin Bench rules prevent contradictory tags.

Poster Best Pick is presentation-only: eligible resolved players must have frozen ADP or Draft HQ rank, clear positive value, and Draft Score at or above the league first quartile. Selection combines 50% value-versus-ADP percentile, 30% value-versus-Draft-HQ-rank percentile, and 20% Draft Score percentile, renormalizing only when a reference is missing. This prevents a low-quality final-round pick from winning on lateness alone. Biggest Reach is the most negative overall-pick-versus-ADP difference, using frozen Draft HQ rank only when ADP is missing; roster fit, minutes, confidence, and positional need are excluded.

`reports/draft_grades_2627_poster_audit.csv` provides one row per manager with Starting XI Minutes and coverage, starter-outlook counts, callout values, and all five group ranks for manual review.
